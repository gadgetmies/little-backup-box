What will happen

An SD card is inserted.Any card reader or USB card counts.
1
Back up the card to the USB SSD. Files are copied (the card is left untouched) and compared by checksum. Skipped if the SSD has less than 10 GB free. About 6 min for 31 GB at typical card-reader speed.
2
When step 1 succeeds, two jobs run at the same time:
2a Generate previews on the USB SSD for the 1 284 files just copied. 2b Upload 2048 px previews of those files to Dropbox. Waits up to 30 min for internet; fails after that.
✓
Done.Nothing runs if step 1 fails — there is no “on failure” step. You still get the failure message.
